## Before you call it done

The full edition's `verify` is not here. Check by hand, by reading the files:

- `references/checklist.md`, every line.
- `references/tells.md`, `references/copy-tells.md`: no scaffold copy left
  (`[[`), at most one italic accent phrase, no invented counts, scores,
  testimonials or logos.
- One `<h1>`; `alt`, `width` and `height` on every image; visible focus;
  content visible without JS; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected; no `#fff`,
  `#000` or zero-chroma grey.
- `references/security.md` before any deploy: no secret in the source, and the
  headers it lists.

Then look at the page at 360 and 1600 px if you can render it, write your two
sentences and stop. Never say an audit, a render check or a verify ran.
