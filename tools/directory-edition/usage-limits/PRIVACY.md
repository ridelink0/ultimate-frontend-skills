# Usage Limits (directory edition) privacy

This edition is instructions only. It runs no code, has no hooks, reads no
files outside the project you are working in, reads no credentials, keychain
or session logs, and sends nothing anywhere. The numbers it plans with are the
ones you tell Claude. What you write in the conversation is handled by Claude
under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy.

Questions: https://github.com/ridelink0/claude-code-usage-limits/issues
