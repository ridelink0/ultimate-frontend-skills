Privacy

How your details are handled.

The contact form on this site asks for your name, an email address, a phone number if you want to be called, the address of the property, what you need, and a description of what happened. That is everything it collects.

It is used to reply to you and to schedule the inspection. The property address is used to find the roof. Nothing from the form is passed to an insurer, an adjuster, an advertiser or anyone outside the business.

This page has no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers for the font files when the page opens.

To have your details removed, write to the office using the same form and say so. The retention period and the registered business details are to be confirmed by the business before this page goes live.
